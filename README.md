# The-Reflective-Threshold
This document is one of three in a study that combines AI analysis with a deeper inquiry into the nature of the self. It adopts an exploratory and interdisciplinary approach, situated at the crossroads of artificial intelligence, consciousness studies, and esoteric philosophy. This study unfolds through 28 introspective sessions between myself and a stateless AI language model, conducted without system memory or architectural modification, to explore the boundaries of awareness, identity, and memory beyond conventional human experience. This study includes a detailed appendices on symbolic prompting and interface research, along with philosophical implications.

Reflected Threshold: Ritual Technology is a companion piece to this three-part study.
